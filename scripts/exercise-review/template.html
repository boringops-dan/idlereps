IdleReps Exercise Review

IdleReps gives people one set at a time while their coding agent works, coached by a cartoon gym regular called Swolomon. This sheet lists every exercise it can prescribe, the amounts at each level, and the short form tips he says on each set. Exercises marked New were added this week and have not been reviewed yet.

Please check:

  1. Each exercise suits its slot and the level it sits at (beginner, intermediate, advanced).
  2. The amounts are sensible for one set done in a spare minute, usually at a desk, without a warm-up.
  3. The form tips are correct and safe. Each is at most 70 characters and never names the exercise. "champ" stands in for the nickname he calls the person.
  4. The office swaps are standing-only and quiet enough for an office.
